# Report Archetypes

Read this when choosing structure, depth, tone, and output format for a report.

## Archetype Router

| Archetype | Use for | Reader question | Default structure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive memo | Founder/leadership decisions, board notes, short recommendations | What should we decide or do? | Summary, recommendation, evidence, tradeoffs, next steps |
| Research report | Market, product, policy, academic-style research | What is true, and how do we know? | Abstract, method, findings, analysis, limitations, references |
| Consulting/market report | Market sizing, competitive intelligence, consumer insights, brand analysis | What is the strategic implication? | Executive summary, market context, findings, strategic analysis, recommendations |
| Investment memo | Public/private company analysis, due diligence, value-investing notes | Is this investable, at what risk? | Thesis, business quality, financials, valuation, risks, variant view, decision |
| Business/data report | KPI, funnel, operations, cohort, growth, analytics reporting | What changed, why, and what action follows? | Key metrics, trend analysis, drivers, anomalies, recommendations, appendix |
| Technical specification | Systems, product requirements, API behavior, implementation decisions | What must be built and constrained? | Goals, non-goals, requirements, architecture, interfaces, edge cases, validation |
| Whitepaper | Public or semi-public explanatory document | Why should the reader believe this point of view? | Position, context, framework, evidence, examples, implications, references |
| Status report | Weekly/monthly updates, milestones, project health | Are we on track? | Summary, progress, blockers, risks, decisions needed, next period |
| Policy/compliance report | Security, privacy, legal, audit, governance reviews | What is compliant, risky, or unresolved? | Scope, controls/criteria, findings, severity, evidence, remediation, appendix |

## Structure Rules

- For decision reports, put the recommendation before the evidence dump.
- For research reports, expose method and limitations early enough that the reader can calibrate trust.
- For public-facing whitepapers, keep claims conservative and sources visible.
- For technical specs, avoid marketing language; define exact behavior, interfaces, constraints, and acceptance checks.
- For investment memos, separate fact, interpretation, and decision. Include a variant view, not only the bullish thesis.

## Tone Rules

- Executive memo: direct, compressed, action-oriented.
- Research report: careful, source-aware, explicit about uncertainty.
- Consulting report: structured, implication-led, but do not over-polish weak evidence.
- Technical spec: precise, testable, unambiguous.
- Chinese report: natural long-form Chinese with concrete headings, not literal translations of English consulting headings.

# Chinese Report Quality Rubric

Use this checklist before finalizing a Chinese report, whitepaper, or PDF-ready document.

## Structure

Pass when:
- The report has a clear summary, body, evidence, risks/assumptions, and next steps.
- Headings are specific and useful.
- Readers can scan the document from the table of contents and summary blocks.

Fix when:
- The report is a long essay with no executive summary.
- Sections repeat the same point under different headings.
- Heading depth becomes chaotic.

## Chinese Readability

Pass when:
- Chinese prose is natural, direct, and not machine-translated.
- Paragraphs are short enough for deep reading.
- Line length, line height, and spacing are comfortable.
- Punctuation, units, dates, and labels are native to Chinese usage.

Fix when:
- English section labels remain in a Chinese report.
- Long sentences copy English syntax.
- Tables or captions use mixed English labels without reason.

## Evidence And Sources

Pass when:
- Important claims have sources, assumptions, or limitations.
- Figures and tables have Chinese captions and source notes.
- Dataset names, query IDs, and technical handles are preserved.

Fix when:
- Charts appear without source/time window/口径.
- A polished conclusion overstates weak evidence.
- Technical identifiers were translated or normalized incorrectly.

## Layout And Output

Pass when:
- The document can be printed/exported without broken page breaks.
- Tables, figures, and captions stay together.
- The layout supports scanning and deep reading.

Fix when:
- A heading is stranded at the page bottom.
- A figure/table splits from its caption.
- Decorative styling harms readability or print output.

# Evidence And Methodology

Read this before drafting claims, methods, citations, data notes, or recommendations.

## Evidence Map

Before writing the final report, create a lightweight internal map:

| Claim | Evidence | Source type | Strength | Gap/action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core conclusion or section claim | URL, dataset, document, quote, screenshot, query, or user-provided assumption | Primary, secondary, internal, illustrative | Strong, medium, weak, unknown | Cite, soften, remove, or mark as assumption |

Do not include this full map in the final report unless useful. Use it to keep the report honest.

## Source Hierarchy

Prefer sources in this order when available:

1. Primary data, original filings, official docs, first-party datasets, source documents.
2. Reputable secondary analysis with clear methodology.
3. News, commentary, blogs, or market maps.
4. User assumptions or illustrative examples.

If the report relies on lower-strength evidence, say so plainly in the methods, notes, or limitation section.

## Claim Policy

- Every important claim needs evidence, a cited source, a visible assumption, or an explicit caveat.
- Numerical claims need source, time window, unit, segment, and calculation method where relevant.
- Causal claims require stronger support than descriptive claims.
- Forecasts should name drivers and uncertainty, not present a single number as fact.
- If data is missing, write `暂无可靠数据支持` or `待补证据` in Chinese reports instead of fabricating.

## Methodology Section

Use a short methodology section for research, investment, market, and data reports:

- 数据来源
- 时间范围
- 样本与筛选口径
- 计算方法
- 局限性

Keep it readable. A methodology section should increase trust, not look like legal padding.

## Citation Styles

- Business memo: inline source notes and short appendix source list.
- Research report: footnotes or end references.
- Public whitepaper: readable endnotes with publication/source names.
- Technical spec: link exact docs, tickets, commits, API references, schemas, or test fixtures.
- Chinese reports: use Chinese labels such as `来源：`、`注：`、`参考资料：`.
